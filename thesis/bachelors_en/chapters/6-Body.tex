\section{Methods}

\subsection{Image representation and FRQI encoding}
Grayscale test images are square arrays of side length $N \in \{4,8,16\}$ (uint8 intensities). FRQI~\cite{Le2011FRQI} encodes each pixel amplitude into one qubit angle with a position register of $m = 2\log_2 N$ qubits so that $N_{\mathrm{pix}} = N^2 = 2^m$ addresses cover all pixels. The implementation in \texttt{src/frqi.py} uses angles $\theta_p = (\pi/2)\cdot (\mathrm{intensity}_p/255)$ consistent with Qiskit \texttt{Ry}, and orders the position register with the \textbf{color qubit as the least significant bit}, matching \texttt{src/improved.py}.

\subsection{QHED baseline and classical Sobel}
The quantum Hadamard edge detector (QHED)~\cite{Yao2017QHED,QiskitTextbookQuantumEdgeDetection} applies a 2D fast Walsh--Hadamard transform, uses the magnitude spectrum as a contrast proxy, inverts to the spatial domain, and applies a classical $3\times 3$ Sobel magnitude (\texttt{src/qhed.py}). Classical Sobel on the original image is the reference edge map.

\subsection{Structural FRQI preparation: naive vs v-chain MCX}
We compare two gate-level preparations of the same ideal FRQI state:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Naive decomposition:} per-pixel address compare, \texttt{MCXGate(..., mode="noancilla")}, controlled \texttt{cry($2\theta_p$)} on the color qubit, then uncompute~\cite{Zindorf2024EfficientMCG}.
  \item \textbf{V-chain path:} identical logic with \texttt{mode="v-chain"}, reusing up to $m-2$ dirty ancillas~\cite{Vale2023MCSU2,Azevedo2024LowDepthMCG}.
\end{itemize}
Full naive transpilation is practical only at $4\times 4$; for $8\times 8$ and $16\times 16$ we report \texttt{struct\_naive\_slice\_scaled} by transpiling one representative slice and multiplying depth/CX by $N_{\mathrm{pix}}$ (documented in \texttt{thesis/design/resource\_and\_complexity.md}).

\subsection{Noise model and image metrics}
Noisy experiments use Qiskit Aer \texttt{density\_matrix} simulation with depolarizing errors on one- and two-qubit gates (\texttt{src/noise\_models.py}). After simulation, the state is partially traced to the FRQI data register and compared to the clean image using PSNR, SSIM (scikit-image), and state fidelity.

\subsection{Readout mitigation}
For shot-based readout, we estimate a $2\times 2$ confusion matrix on the color qubit, apply regularized inversion, and adjust measured $p_1$ (\texttt{thesis/design/readout\_mitigation.md}; demo \texttt{scripts/readout\_mitigation\_shot\_sweep.py}).

\section{Experimental setup}

\subsection{Simulator-only scope}
\textbf{All results in this chapter are simulator-only.} No IBM Quantum hardware data are included. Week~8 of the project plan recorded that hardware access was not available; evidence uses Aer statevector/density-matrix backends and synthetic noise grids.

\subsection{Data and software}
Datasets: \texttt{data/test\_images/test\_\{4,8,16\}x\{4,8,16\}.npy}. Software stack: \texttt{requirements.txt} (Qiskit~1.x, Aer, NumPy, SciPy, pandas, scikit-image). Three experiment tracks: (1)~ideal FRQI+QHED baseline, (2)~structural resource comparison, (3)~noisy reconstruction with optional readout mitigation.

\subsection{Reproducible commands}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Demo (FRQI + QHED):} \texttt{python scripts/frqi\_qhed\_sobel.py}
  \item \textbf{Structural resources:} \texttt{python scripts/frqi\_structural\_resources.py}
  \item \textbf{Noisy FRQI sweep:} \texttt{python scripts/noisy\_frqi\_sweep.py} (skips $16\times 16$ v-chain unless \texttt{--allow-heavy-dm})
  \item \textbf{Noisy recon $\rightarrow$ QHED edges:} \texttt{python scripts/noisy\_recon\_qhed\_edge\_metrics.py}

  \item \textbf{Regenerate thesis tables:} \texttt{python scripts/build\_results\_tables.py --require-figures}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Transpiled structural cost vs image size}
\Cref{fig:cx-vs-n,fig:depth-vs-n,tab:structural_resources} summarize transpiled CX and depth at optimization level~3 (basis \texttt{cx,rz,sx}). At $16\times 16$, v-chain uses 16~qubits with 11\,663~CX versus 114\,176~CX for the scaled naive estimate (10~qubits, extrapolated from one slice). The v-chain layout trades additional ancillas for far lower two-qubit volume---the dominant NISQ cost in many superconducting layouts~\cite{Geng2023ImprovedFRQI}.

\subsection{Edge metrics after noisy reconstruction}
The primary noisy-endpoint metric is \textbf{edge SSIM} between QHED applied to the noisy reconstruction and Sobel on the clean original (\texttt{noisy\_recon\_qhed\_edges.csv}). Paired naive versus v-chain comparisons across eight $(\mathrm{image},\mathrm{noise\_scale})$ cells with $\mathrm{noise\_scale}>0$ yield median $\Delta$\,edge\_SSIM (vchain$-$naive) $\approx -0.18$ with Wilcoxon $p \approx 0.078$---\textbf{not} strong evidence that v-chain uniformly improves edges under noise. Some cells favor v-chain (e.g.\ $8\times 8$ at scale $0.2$), others favor naive; at $16\times 16$ v-chain edge columns are \texttt{NaN} because the v-chain noisy run was not completed.

Qualitative edge maps are available as \texttt{outputs/noisy\_recon\_qhed\_edges\_<image>.png} when the edge-metrics script is run.

\subsection{Readout mitigation slice}
\Cref{tab:readout_summary,fig:readout-mitigation} summarize ten random seeds on \texttt{test\_4x4}. Mean raw $p_1 \approx 0.515$ shifts to $\approx 0.499$ after mitigation (mean $\Delta \approx -0.015$). Paired Wilcoxon on mitigated$-$raw differences gives $p \approx 0.002$, supporting that calibration inversion moves estimates in the expected direction on this synthetic readout model---a proof-of-concept, not a hardware benchmark.

\subsection{Limitations}
Results do not include device calibration drift, spatial correlations, or crosstalk. $16\times 16$ v-chain noisy and edge rows are often missing due to $4^{n_q}$ density-matrix memory. Statistical tests are exploratory with small paired sample sizes. Claims about edge quality under noise must remain conditional on image and noise scale.

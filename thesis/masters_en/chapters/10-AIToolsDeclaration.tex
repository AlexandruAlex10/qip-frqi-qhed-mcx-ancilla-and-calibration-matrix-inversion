\chapter{Declaration on the use of artificial intelligence tools}\label{ch:ai-declaration}
\thispagestyle{pagestyle}

In accordance with Art.~3(c) of the dissertation methodology, this chapter declares the artificial intelligence (AI) tools used while preparing this thesis, the purpose for which each was used, and the exact sections in which their assistance was applied. The intellectual content, the research design, the experiments, and the conclusions are the author's own; AI tools were used only as assistive aids and every AI-assisted passage was reviewed, edited, and verified by the author.

\section{Types of tools used}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Large language model assistants} (general-purpose conversational LLMs) for documentation search, explanation of concepts, and language editing.
  \item \textbf{AI-assisted coding tools} (an LLM-backed code assistant integrated in the editor) for drafting and refactoring Python scripts and \LaTeX{} fragments.
\end{itemize}

\section{Purpose of use}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Documentation and literature orientation:} locating and summarizing background references on FRQI, QHED, multi-controlled gate synthesis, and NISQ noise, which were then read and cited from the primary sources.
  \item \textbf{Rephrasing and proofreading:} improving clarity, grammar, and flow of English prose and the Romanian summary, without changing the technical claims.
  \item \textbf{Code generation and refactoring:} drafting boilerplate for the experiment scripts (circuit construction, transpilation accounting, plotting, CSV/JSON manifest handling) and \LaTeX{} table scaffolding, all of which were tested and corrected by the author.
\end{itemize}

\section{Sections where AI assistance was used}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Language editing} was applied across the abstract (\Cref{ch:related} background prose), the Romanian \emph{Rezumat}, the Introduction, and the Conclusions.
  \item \textbf{Documentation orientation} supported the related-work survey in \Cref{ch:related} and the positioning discussion; all retained citations were verified against the original publications.
  \item \textbf{Code assistance} contributed to the Python scripts referenced in \Cref{ch:methods} (\texttt{frqi\_structural\_resources.py}, \texttt{noisy\_recon\_qhed\_edge\_metrics.py}, \texttt{readout\_mitigation\_shot\_sweep.py}, and the table-build utilities) and to the generated result tables included in \Cref{ch:results} and the appendices.
\end{itemize}

No AI tool was used to fabricate experimental data, results, or references. All numerical results derive from the versioned scripts and CSV/JSON manifests in the project repository, and the author takes full responsibility for the content of this thesis.
